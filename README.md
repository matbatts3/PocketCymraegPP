Pocket Cymraeg Privacy Policy
Privacy Policy for PocketCymraeg 
Last Updated: September 8, 2026 
Welcome to PocketCymraeg ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting 
your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is 
collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and related 
services (collectively, the "App"). 
Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy 
Policy, please do not access or use the App. 
1. Information We Collect 
We collect information to deliver tailored language-learning features and improve 
your overall experience: 
• User-Generated Inputs: Scenarios, schedules, prompts, notes, and free-text 
queries you submit to generate phrases or translations. 
• App Preferences: Your chosen dialect (North Wales / Gogledd vs. South 
Wales / De), audience/age tone selections, saved/favourited phrases, and 
custom vocabulary lists. 
• Technical & Device Information: Non-personally identifiable technical 
information automatically collected when using the App, including device 
model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, language 
settings, and general diagnostic/crash logs. 
• Account Information (if applicable): If you register an account, we may 
collect your email address and authentication credentials. If you access the 
App as a guest, no direct contact credentials are required. 
2. How We Use Your Information 
We use the collected information for the following purposes: 
• Delivering Contextual Output: Generating dynamic Welsh phrases, phonetic 
breakdowns, anticipated replies, and translations tailored to your specific 
daily scenarios. 
• Personalisation: Remembering your preferred dialect, tone presets, and 
pinned "Pocket Picks" across sessions. 
• App Maintenance & Analytics: Monitoring stability, diagnosing bugs, fixing 
performance bottlenecks, and assessing which features deliver the highest 
value to learners. 
• Safety & Integrity: Preventing abuse, misuse, or violations of our terms of 
service. 
3. Third-Party Services & AI Processing 
PocketCymraeg utilizes third-party infrastructure and artificial intelligence 
providers to process natural language queries and support App performance: 
• AI Service Providers (e.g., Google Cloud / Gemini API): Your text prompts and 
scenarios are transmitted securely to our AI infrastructure providers solely 
to generate language output. These inputs are processed in accordance with 
enterprise data protection standards and are not used to publicly identify 
you. 
• Analytics and Crash Reporting: We may use tools like Google Firebase 
Crashlytics to monitor application performance and stability. 
• App Store Platforms: In-app transactions and billing (if applicable) are 
handled directly by Google Play or Apple App Store, subject to their 
respective terms and privacy policies. 
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for targeted 
marketing or advertising. 
4. Data Retention 
• User-submitted scenario inputs are processed transiently to generate real
time phrases and are not permanently tied to your identity on public servers. 
• Locally saved items (such as pinned phrases, offline decks, and 
preferences) remain stored on your device until you delete them or clear the 
App’s data. 
• Technical diagnostics and crash telemetry are retained only as long as 
necessary to ensure App reliability. 
5. Children’s Privacy 
PocketCymraeg is designed to help parents, families, and adult learners introduce 
Welsh into daily family life. The App does not knowingly collect personal 
identifiable information directly from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in 
the UK/EEA). If you believe personal information from a child has been 
inadvertently submitted, please contact us immediately so we can remove it. 
6. Security of Your Information 
We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security 
measures to protect your personal information. However, no electronic 
transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be 
guaranteed to be 100% secure. 
7. Your Data Rights 
Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the UK GDPR or EU GDPR), you have 
specific rights regarding your personal data, including: 
• The right to access the personal information we hold about you. 
• The right to request correction or deletion of your data. 
• The right to withdraw consent where data processing is based on consent. 
• The right to object to or restrict certain processing activities. 
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address provided below. 
8. Changes to This Privacy Policy 
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our 
features or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with 
an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy 
periodically.
